2 · Objections

When price is challenged, I change the scope, not the price

"Your renewal is higher than last year"

The structure I use in my replies, from my 2026 sent emails

1 · Show the historyAttach the previous order form and explain what changed: fixed price ended, list price moved, usage changed.
2 · Ask, don't offer"Which price point would work for you?" No discount named.
3 · Trade scope, not priceFewer seats, a different tier, or a different term. Every change gets a fresh quote.
4 · Match only on evidenceA published or competing price, and only after an internal check.

When an objection resolved, it was usually through a seat, tier or term change, not a price cut. Example: the wealth manager deal below.
