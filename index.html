Rules I changed from my own data

  • Increases over 20% lost more: now capped by how close an account is to list.
  • Lost partner-led renewals highlighted gaps in customer contact. I now contact customers directly.
  • Low-usage accounts churned: usage check 90 days before every renewal.

Net revenue retention: 90.5%

165 renewals, April to September 2026, churn included

My book is mostly small businesses buying through resellers. Most of the lost value sat in two places: renewals under US$10K, and partner-led renewals where no one at Nitro had spoken to the customer directly (26% of lost renewal value). Both are now covered by the rules above: a direct contact 90 days out, and a usage check before every renewal.
