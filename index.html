4 · Rebalance

I test my own approach and change it

A monthly scorecard checks my sales claims against about 550 renewals of CRM data. When the data says I am wrong, the rule changes.

Large increases lost more

Above 20%, win rate fell to 69%.

Change: cap the increase by how close the account already is to list price.

Partner-led renewals leaked

Deals with no direct customer contact lost more value.

Change: I contact the customer myself, even when a reseller leads.

Small renewals retained less

US$2K–10K renewals retained below my larger ones.

Change: one direct contact 90 days out for that band.
