How do you run your week?

A fixed rhythm, supported by agents I built, so no renewal starts late and every call starts prepared.

My week

Monday

Priority list: renewals tiered, first emails drafted for the top tier.

Tuesday

Forecast numbers and deal review with my director.

Wednesday

One status email per distributor, listing every open deal.

Thursday

Customer and partner calls, trials, quotes.

Friday

Pipeline summary and weekly results.

Every day

Morning

Brief for each meeting: deal, contract, history, questions. Reply drafts for overnight mail.

During the day

Renewals opened about 60 days out. One ask per email.

End of day

Next steps written into the CRM for every deal touched.

Q7

How do you work with other teams?

My deals usually involve other groups beyond the customer. I keep each one briefed before it matters.

Resellers and distributors

Briefed before any customer meeting. Quotes through the distributor, PO chased with the reseller.

Solutions engineers

Trials, SSO tests and integration scoping, booked with a decision date.

Support and success

Technical issues routed to the right team with context, so I stay on the commercial side.

Finance and deal desk

Approvals asked once, with the reason and what we get in return.

Buyers I sell to: IT managers and engineers, finance and procurement, with security involved in most deals.
